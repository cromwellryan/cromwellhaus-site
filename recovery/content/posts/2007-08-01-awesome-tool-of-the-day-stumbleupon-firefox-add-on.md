The other day I happened upon the [Firefox Add-On page](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/) and decided to give the recommended Add-On a try.  I've been infatuated ever since.

Though there's an inordinate number of buttons on the default toolbar layout, [StumbleUpon](http://www.stumbleupon.com/) is pretty simple: You/others browse the web and when you find something you like you give it a thumbs-up [![image](http://cromwellhaus.com/blogs/ryanc/WindowsLiveWriter/AwesomeTooloftheDayStumbleUponFirefoxAdd_8956/image_thumb_2.png)](http://cromwellhaus.com/blogs/ryanc/WindowsLiveWriter/AwesomeTooloftheDayStumbleUponFirefoxAdd_8956/image_2.png) or a thumbs-down [![image](http://cromwellhaus.com/blogs/ryanc/WindowsLiveWriter/AwesomeTooloftheDayStumbleUponFirefoxAdd_8956/image_thumb_3.png)](http://cromwellhaus.com/blogs/ryanc/WindowsLiveWriter/AwesomeTooloftheDayStumbleUponFirefoxAdd_8956/image_3.png) if you don't.  Then there's the magic button [![image](http://cromwellhaus.com/blogs/ryanc/WindowsLiveWriter/AwesomeTooloftheDayStumbleUponFirefoxAdd_8956/image_thumb_4.png)](http://cromwellhaus.com/blogs/ryanc/WindowsLiveWriter/AwesomeTooloftheDayStumbleUponFirefoxAdd_8956/image_4.png) which randomly picks pages from the submitted pages that are recommended (thumbs-up).  Their pitch says it pretty well...*"Channel surf the internet."*

I warn you, it is very addictive, but if you choose topics you are actually interested in, you'll find quite a few cool and relevant sites.  [Check out](http://cromwellryan.stumbleupon.com) my recommended sites so far..

[Get the StumbleUpon Add-On](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/138)
