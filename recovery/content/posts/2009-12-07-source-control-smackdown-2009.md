With all the hubbub around Git, TFS 2010, and others over the last year or so, the [Dayton .Net Developers Group](http://daytondevgroup.net/) is hosting a panel event this Wednesday, December 9th, titled [Source Control Smackdown 2009](http://daytondevgroup.net/content/UpcomingMeetings.aspx).  I have been lucky enough to be selected to represent TFS in a clash against SVN ([Justin Kohnen](http://bouncetadiss.blogspot.com/)) and Git ([Leon Gersing](http://fallenrogue.com/)).  Please come down and share your experiences, learn some facts and enjoy the banter that will undoubtedly ensue.

Find directions, contact information, panel member bios and other details on the [Dayton .Net Developers Group site](http://daytondevgroup.net/content/UpcomingMeetings.aspx).
