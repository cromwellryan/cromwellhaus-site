It looks like [Mono](http://www.mono-project.com/ "Mono Project") will finally be a valid option for deploying applications. Red Hat has [announced](http://www.eweek.com/article2/0,1895,1909674,00.asp "Mono to Be Included in Fedora Core 5") that it will include [Mono](http://www.mono-project.com/ "Mono Project") in its Fedora Core 5. Does that mean it will be in [Red Hat](http://www.redhat.com/ "RedHat") as well? I couldn't tell you since I don't follow the Linux distributions to closely, but that would definitly be impressive. I personally run [Ubuntu](http://www.ubuntu.com/ "Ubuntu") and, being a Linux novice, find it to be the closest thing to a "Mom-usable" Linux OS out there. Then again, I don't follow the Linux distributions to closely.
