Dayton Code & Coffee is a semi-coherent group of software developers engaging in a caffeinated learning experience.  Focusing on topics which diverge from our routine, this collective was organized by the brain trust [@derekhubbard](http://twitter.com/derekhubbard) and [@cschroll](http://twitter.com/cschroll).

When: Every Wednesday @ 7:00am until your cup is empty or your brain is full

Where: Caribou Coffee on Far Hills/SR 48 in Kettering ([Directions](http://www.bing.com/maps/OneClickDirections.aspx?rtp=%7epos.qm95827zm2zk_5839+Far+Hills+Ave%2c+Dayton%2c+OH_Caribou+Coffee_(937)+432-9326_a_YN693x35198887&mkt=en-us&FORM=LLMP))
