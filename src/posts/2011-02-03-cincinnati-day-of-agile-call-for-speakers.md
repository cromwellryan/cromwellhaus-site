I’ve had the pleasure of working with the group organizing Cincinnati Day of Agile.  About a week ago we confirm our facility and date: March 26th, 2011 at the [Savanna Center](http://savannahcenter.com) in West Chester, Ohio.  We expect quite a jump this year, going over 300 attendees.

We are currently accepting topics for both the Beginner and Advanced tracks.  If you have any experiences with Agile techniques either good, bad or wild this is your chance to help others learn from those experiences.  Whether it’s the impact of a transparent backlog on your business, the passionate arguments that came from a collaborating team, or the impact of coding practices on your velocity over time, people want to know what you have to say.  Don’t be shy.  Submit your topics at <http://cincydayofagile.org> soon!

Looks forward to seeing everyone there.  The facility is beautiful and the discussions are going to be awesome, so don’t miss out.
