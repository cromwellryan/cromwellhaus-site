[Steve Gentile](http://blogger.forgottenskies.com/) recently had a thinking out loud moment by posting the Agile Manifest as a reminder to himself of what he values.  It’s really important to constantly put these types of reminders in front of ourselves as we work in environments that don’t necessarily breed best practices.  The contractual obligations of the business world definitely don’t lend to “welcoming change.”  Often our focus on “getting paid” blinds us to the goal of “continuous delivery of valuable software.”

It is a test of our dedication that we continually reassess our values and attempt to embed them more deeply in our daily activities..
