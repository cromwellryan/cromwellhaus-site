I realize this isn't the MiniMVC post I promised, but that one is a monster and I wanted to feel like I accomplished something this evening.

[![image](http://cromwellhaus.com/blogphotos/SoWhatsYourWindowsExperienceScore_87C2/image_thumb.png)](http://cromwellhaus.com/blogphotos/SoWhatsYourWindowsExperienceScore_87C2/image.png)Last week I was forced to replace a dying video card which was a piece-of-junk when I bought it, but, well, I'm cheap.  I picked up an [NVIDIA GeForce 8600 GT](http://www.nvidia.com/object/geforce_8600.html) and while not *the* best, it's more than I'll need.

When I built this rig, I picked up about the best available at the time.  Looking at my latest Windows Experience Score, I'm sort of regretting not picking up the Intel 6800 Core 2 Duo, though it was significantly more expensive than the 6600 I  did choose.  I guess I can't fault the decision based on the facts at the time.  That said, my [Dell M1530](http://www.dell.com/content/products/productdetails.aspx/xpsnb_m1530?c=us&cs=19&l=en&s=dhs&~ck=mn) has the 6800 and I swear it's a friggin' beast.

What's your score?
