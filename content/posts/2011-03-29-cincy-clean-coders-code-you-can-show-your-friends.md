What would a Monday following an [Agile conference](http://www.cincydayofagile.org/) be [without the announcement](http://twitter.com/cromwellryan/statuses/52408693400154112) of a new group focused on quality practices!

I’m happy to announce that [Cincy Clean Coders](http://cincycleancoders.com/) is really going to happen.  Details can be found at <http://cincycleancoders.com>, but suffice it to say the inaugural meeting will be held April 7th at 6pm in the [Max Train](http://www.maxtrain.com) facilities in Mason, Ohio.

[Mark Haskamp](http://twitter.com/markhaskamp) was strong armed brave enough to offer to go first with me.  I’ll update the site with the topics for the first meeting soon.

**Big Thanks** to [Ernie Stormann](http://twitter.com/EStormann) and [Parag Joshi](http://twitter.com/ilovethexbox) who helped push this through.  And a big kudos to the rest of Twitter for being excited about a topic like this.  It says a lot about where our industry wants to be.
