One of the things we are using here at [SDS](http://www.sds-consulting.com/) for our TFS installation and projects is the [Scrum for Team System](http://www.scrumforteamsystem.com/) which provides a new Sharepoint Portal layout, Reporting Services reports and graphs, Work Item Templates, and an awesome [Process Guidance reference](http://www.scrumforteamsystem.com/processguidance/v2/Process/Process.aspx).

In this screencast, I try to point out some of these features and how to get to them.  It's short (just over 2 minutes) and I'll dive into each as we continue our storyline.

Shoutout to Camtasia which is sooo simple to use.
