[![sb-logo-buildright-dark](/images/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/logo-buildright-dark.png)](/images/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/logo-buildright-dark.png)Back when [I started Cincy Clean Coders](/2011/03/cincy-clean-coders-code-you-can-show-your-friends/ "Cincy Clean Coders – Code You Can Show Your Friends"), it felt like the Dayton community was nearly nonexistent.  It seemed like I needed to head down to the big city to attract a community of Clean Coders.

Fast forward 2 years… the Cincy group has stopped meeting, [Dayton Clean Coders](http://daytoncleancoders.com "Dayton Clean Coders") is going strong (with [a bad-ass logo](/images/external/content.screencast.com/users/cromwellryan/folders/Snagit/media/fe56c6ba-1631-427c-acd6-bd5ef4f03c42/10.30.2013-15.19.png)), an [Elixir](http://elixir-lang.org "Elixir") Group is forming, and [GemCityJS](http://gemcityjs.com/ "GemCityJS") is growing.  There’s [Build Guild](http://dayton.buildguild.org/) and others as well.  The Dayton tech scene is thriving!

It turns out the people at the center of making much of this happen are also doing great things for the web at [Sparkbox](http://seesparkbox.com/ "SparkBox") by leading the world in responsive web design.

Starting next week I’m excited to join the awesome Sparkbox team, focusing hard on making the web and Dayton community an even more amazing place!

Between the platform shift to open source, Ruby, Rails, etc. and the challenge of working with some of the most passion devs I’ve met, this looks to be an exciting challenge just to keep up!

#### Microsoft ALM & MVP Community

This move likely means stepping away from the Microsoft ALM & MVP community.  These have been some of the most passionate and hard working people I’ve met.  They give an immense amount of their time towards helping build great software.

While TFS has it’s fair share of critics, they’re moving in [a great direction](http://blogs.msdn.com/b/bharry/archive/2013/01/30/git-init-vs.aspx "Git Init VS"), [fast](http://blogs.msdn.com/b/bharry/archive/2012/08/28/tfs-shipping-cadence.aspx "TFS Shipping Cadence"). And with a leader like Brian Harry making comments like this in private email lists…

> I’m not horribly worried about being copied.  If we can make the whole world provide better services, I’m happy.

…I have no doubt that set of tools will be in a great place in the coming years.
